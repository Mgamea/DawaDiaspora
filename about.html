Our story

Language is how a diaspora stays home

DawaDiaspora was built for the sons and daughters of migrants who want their children to speak the language their grandparents dreamed in — Kinyarwanda, Swahili, Arabic, and beyond.

Our mission

Millions of families abroad watch a language slip away one generation at a time. We build lesson libraries — readable, listenable, watchable — so that distance from home doesn't have to mean distance from the mother tongue.

How we teach

Every course pairs a written guide with native audio and video, so you can learn the way that suits you — on a commute, at a kitchen table, or with your children beside you.

Built with the community

Our lessons are written and recorded by native speakers from the communities we serve, not generic courseware — because accent, rhythm, and context are half of any language.
